ABOUT THE EXPLORER

A familiar place.
A fresh perspective.

This Atlas venue explorer uses the actual campus illustration and original panoramic photographs from Montego Bay Convention Centre’s official virtual tour. The old tour relied on Flash; these images are presented in a modern 360° viewer.

Images are archival and their capture dates are not specified. They may differ from the venue today. Hotspots and building outlines are approximate visual guides, not survey boundaries, routes or live availability. No interior image is supplied where one has not been verified.
